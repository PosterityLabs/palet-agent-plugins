description: A finished Palet design ends with the card from get_link, once
alwaysApply: true
---

When you finish a design in Palet, or stop because you need the person's input or can't go on, call `get_link` once and end your final reply with the `card` it returns, exactly as given. Its description says what to pass. Don't share Palet links before then, and never write one yourself.
